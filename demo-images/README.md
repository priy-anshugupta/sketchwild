# Synthetic upload examples

These images were AI-generated for manually checking the app. They are not real outdoor photographs, participant drawings, or held-out evaluation evidence.

Choose **Leaves** in SketchWild. Upload `01-leaf-reference.png` as the real-subject slot and `02-pencil-sketch.png` as the paper-sketch slot. Both files are under the 8 MB upload limit.

The reference has a toothed edge and many side veins. The sketch has a smooth edge and only a few side veins. Useful grounded feedback could invite a closer look at one of those differences. Exact wording will vary, and the model can make mistakes. It should not claim the sketch has all the reference's detailed veins or treat the pencil picture as the real leaf.

After testing the UI, repeat the workflow with your own real outdoor subject and paper drawing for the project trial.
